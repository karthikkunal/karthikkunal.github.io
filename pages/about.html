THE APPROACH

Security from the packet to the platform.

My foundation is cybersecurity, and my curiosity doesn’t stop at one specialty. I explore application and API security, vulnerability testing, incident response, digital forensics, threat intelligence, network detection, wireless threats, cloud security, secure infrastructure and cryptography.

My enterprise role at LTIMindtree has deepened my hands-on security-operations experience through Microsoft Defender XDR and Microsoft Defender for Office 365. I investigate suspicious activity, analyze evidence with KQL and message traces, and help make the response clearer and more useful.

Across Go and Python tools, Linux infrastructure, Raspberry Pi prototypes, encryption experiments, distributed intrusion detection and AI-assisted investigations, one idea ties my work together: build defense with evidence, human judgment and sound engineering—not hype.
